# ADR-003 — Tracktion Engine + JUCE as the Native Audio Foundation

Status: accepted

## Context

Corytm needs a native, low-latency audio/MIDI/plugin-hosting foundation. Strudel and DawDreamer were both considered and are explicitly not part of Corytm's runtime/product architecture.

## Decision

Corytm has selected Tracktion Engine + JUCE as one coupled native foundation for the Native Audio Runtime. Tracktion Engine supplies the DAW-level layer: the Edit, tracks, clips, playback graph, transport, automation, plugin model, rendering, and MIDI-related DAW operations. JUCE supplies the lower-level native audio/MIDI/plugin-format/device foundation that stack uses: audio I/O, MIDI I/O, plugin format/device/OS abstractions, buffers, and VST3/AU hosting infrastructure. Recording them as one decision reflects the actual technology choice Corytm has made, not two independently selectable options.

## Consequences

Corytm inherits Tracktion Engine's and JUCE's licensing model, with implications for eventual closed-source distribution — business/licensing context tracked separately, not detailed here. The Native Audio Runtime is structurally coupled to Tracktion Engine's own concepts as the target of ADR-001's projection. Replacing this foundation later would be a major rewrite.

## Alternatives considered

Strudel — evaluated, not adopted. DawDreamer — evaluated, not adopted.
