# Corytm Market Intelligence

Status: canonical home for market intelligence, mutable by design. Unlike `docs/product/strategy.md` and `docs/product/business-model.md`, nothing in this document is itself an approved decision — it is evidence, not doctrine.

## Policy

- This document records meaningful competitor developments, validated public observations, product/market signals, threats, opportunities, strategic hypotheses, and questions worth revisiting.
- Market intelligence informs strategy but does not override approved product strategy (`docs/product/strategy.md`, `docs/product/business-model.md`) by itself. A conflict between an observation here and approved strategy is surfaced, not silently resolved in either direction.
- Competitor behavior does not automatically trigger copycat feature development.
- When a competitor development materially challenges Corytm's positioning, architecture, economics, or roadmap assumptions, the CPO surfaces the implication and proposes a decision if necessary (`CLAUDE.md` §3's CPO Mandate) — through the normal Protected Product Decision path (`docs/product/strategy.md` §18) if the implication reaches that level.
- Not every Task performs market research. It is especially appropriate when planning a major Epic, evaluating a strategic assumption, considering pricing/monetization, assessing a major capability, when a known competitor development may materially change Corytm's position, or when explicitly requested.
- Every entry needs a concrete evidence anchor — an actual observation, event, release, or encountered behavior, dated, with its source named. Speculation is allowed only attached to such an anchor and explicitly hedged ("may," "appears to," "suspected") — never written as settled fact.
- Default is to keep this document short. Stale, disproven, or superseded entries are deleted outright, not archived — Git owns the history.
