Status: done

## Objective

Author `.claude/skills/testing-review/SKILL.md`: a narrow skill that operationalizes `docs/technical/testing.md` (written in TK-033) when tests are added/changed and when a Feature's validation is being planned or closed — without restating the policy document's own content.

## Scope / Out of scope

In scope: `.claude/skills/testing-review/SKILL.md` (new file).

Out of scope: any change to `docs/technical/testing.md` itself; any change to `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/project/format.md`, or `docs/project/plan.md` (a skill inherits those documents' rules per `CLAUDE.md` §11, it does not restate or extend them).

## Acceptance criteria

- The skill points to `docs/technical/testing.md` as ground truth and does not restate its four-tier model, invalidation table, or clean-build triggers.
- Covers, concretely: test placement; selecting appropriate fidelity; recognizing complementary versus duplicate guarantees; updating the invalidation table's input-set when a new cross-subsystem dependency edge is introduced; Tier selection during implementation (targeted inner-loop execution); deciding which subsystem validations have been invalidated (the git-diff-based check, not elapsed time); avoiding ritual duplicate execution (`pytest` → `check-python` → `make check` when nothing changed in between); preserving real IPC/process/native/device-test fidelity and `live_llm`/human-manual evidence boundaries at Feature close.
- Follows this repository's existing skill format and tone (`.claude/skills/ui-ux-review/SKILL.md`): frontmatter `description` only, a ground-truth pointer, a numbered checklist, an "Output" section naming what it feeds into, an "Out of scope" section.
- Explicitly forbids, in its own Out of scope section, inventing a test-result cache/fingerprint/ledger, and forbids lowering fidelity or narrowing acceptance criteria to gain speed.
